Grow Your Potential.

PUPUK Academy is an education-focused digital platform designed to connect students, parents, independent tutors and educators through accessible learning opportunities and educational resources.

We believe every learner deserves the opportunity to grow, explore their potential and develop skills that extend beyond the classroom. Our platform brings together independent tutors offering personalised learning support and a growing collection of educational materials to support students across different academic levels, subjects and learning needs.

What We Offer

Find Independent Tutors

Discover independent tutors across a range of academic subjects and skill areas. Explore tutor profiles, qualifications, experience and areas of expertise to find suitable learning support.

Educational Learning Materials

Access educational resources, worksheets, notes, practice questions and other learning materials designed to support independent learning and academic preparation.

Opportunities for Educators

We provide independent tutors and educators with an opportunity to showcase their expertise, connect with prospective students and expand their reach through our platform.

Our Mission

To make quality educational support and learning resources more accessible by bringing learners and educators together through technology.

Our Vision

To build an inclusive educational ecosystem where learning opportunities are accessible, flexible and empowering for everyone.

Our Approach

We believe education should be personalised, accessible and continuous. By combining independent tutoring opportunities with digital learning resources, PUPUK Academy aims to support learners at every stage of their educational journey.
